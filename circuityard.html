How the used marketplace works

The same three steps for anyone listing or buying second-hand hardware.

01

List your part

Add the part name, condition, and price. It appears in the marketplace instantly for others to browse.

02

Buyer requests it

Interested buyers hit "Buy," which opens a chat with the seller to confirm price and handover.

03

Meet, ship, or pickup

Both sides agree on delivery. Once confirmed, the listing is automatically marked sold.
