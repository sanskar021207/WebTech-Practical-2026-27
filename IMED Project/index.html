How the used side works

The same three steps for anyone listing or buying second-hand hardware.

01

List your part

Fill in the part name, condition, and price. It appears in the marketplace instantly for others to browse.

02

Buyer requests it

Interested buyers hit "Buy," which opens a chat with the seller to confirm price and handover details.

03

Meet, ship, or pickup

Both sides agree on local pickup or shipping. Once confirmed, the listing is marked sold automatically.
