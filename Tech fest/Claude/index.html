FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before registering

Who can attend TechFest?

TechFest is open to all currently enrolled college/university students (undergraduate, graduate, PhD). You'll need a valid student ID for verification at check-in. High school students may attend with a teacher chaperone.

What's included in the registration fee?

Your pass includes: access to all keynotes and tech talks, 3 workshops of your choice, hackathon participation, career fair entry, networking dinner, event swag kit (t-shirt, stickers, notebook), and certificate of participation.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend?

Full refunds available until February 15th. 50% refund until March 1st. No refunds after March 1st, but you can transfer your ticket to another student from your college free of charge.

Do I need a team for the hackathon?

No! You can register solo and we'll match you with a team at the team formation event on Day 1. Teams can be 2-4 people. If you already have a team, register together to stay grouped.

Is there a group discount?

Yes! Groups of 5+ from the same college get 15% off. Groups of 10+ get 20% off. The discount is applied automatically at checkout when you register multiple tickets in one order.

What should I bring?

Laptop + charger, student ID, government-issued ID, reusable water bottle, and comfortable clothes. For hackathon: any hardware you need (we provide basic electronics). Business casual for career fair day.
