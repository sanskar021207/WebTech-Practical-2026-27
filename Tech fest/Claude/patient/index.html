Our Services

Comprehensive Medical Care

Mercy General Hospital provides a full spectrum of healthcare services

Emergency Services

Level 1 Trauma Center open 24/7 with board-certified emergency physicians.

Inpatient Care

200 private rooms with specialized units for cardiac, neuro, and critical care.

Surgical Services

12 state-of-the-art ORs including hybrid suites and robotic surgery platforms.

Maternity & NICU

Family-centered birthing suites with Level III NICU for premature infants.

Diagnostic Imaging

3T MRI, 256-slice CT, PET/CT, 3D mammography, and interventional radiology.

Rehabilitation

Inpatient and outpatient PT, OT, speech therapy, and cardiac rehab programs.
