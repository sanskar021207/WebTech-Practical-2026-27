ABOUT OUR INSTITUTE

Welcome to IMED Pune

Institute of Management and Entrepreneurship Development was established by Bharati Vidyapeeth in 1978. It is a constituent institute of Bharati Vidyapeeth (Deemed to be University), Pune.

IMED focuses on management education, computer applications, research, entrepreneurship and industry-oriented learning.

The institute is spread across approximately four acres and provides academic and instructional facilities for students.
